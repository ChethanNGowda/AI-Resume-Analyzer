# HR & Behavioral Interview Preparation Guide

## The STAR Method Framework
For every behavioral question, structure your response as:
- **Situation**: Set the scene and background context briefly (15-20% of response).
- **Task**: Explain the challenge, objective, or responsibility you had to solve (10-15%).
- **Action**: Describe specific technical, organizational, and leadership actions YOU took (50-60%).
- **Result**: Conclude with quantifiable impact, lessons learned, or project outcome (15-20%).

## High-Frequency Behavioral Questions & Strategies
1. **"Tell me about yourself"**
   - Present -> Past -> Future formula:
     - Present: Current role, major technical strengths and domain focus.
     - Past: Educational foundation and standout projects or work experiences.
     - Future: Why this specific role and company aligns with your next career trajectory.
2. **"Tell me about a time you faced a difficult conflict with a teammate"**
   - Focus on professional alignment, data-driven decisions, mutual respect, and resolution rather than interpersonal complaints.
3. **"What is your greatest technical weakness?"**
   - Pick a genuine, non-fatal area of growth that you are actively taking measurable steps to improve (e.g., public speaking, deep frontend CSS nuances when you are primarily backend).
4. **"Why do you want to join our company?"**
   - Research recent company products, engineering blogs, tech stack choices, or mission statements. Connect your personal projects or interests to their domain.
